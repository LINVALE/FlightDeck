# Multizone control systems — survey

*Produced 2026-08-27 by a 15-agent workflow: nine platform researchers, a completeness
critic, four gap-fillers and a synthesis. Every recommendation is constrained to what
Roon's extension API can actually do.*

## What FlightDeck should match

**1. Anchor discipline: the head is always the room that is actually playing. — do this one first.**
Before any `group_outputs`, decide the head by play state, not by which Face you happen to be on: if a member is playing and the bound zone is not, either promote the playing zone to head, or `transfer_zone(playingZone → headOutput)` first (already the shape of `preset-activation-transaction.ts:151`). **Why:** the measured law is that a non-playing head yields an empty zone with `allowed.play:false` and kills any playing member's music — 5/5 runs, both islands. Every other item here is decoration on a recall that otherwise produces a dead zone. **Effort: small** (logic exists in-repo). **Roon:** `group_outputs(ordered)` head-wins; gate the follow-up play on `allowed.play`, and treat `allowed.play === false` after a confirmed group as a failed recall to report, not a group to keep.

**2. Stage the whole membership, commit once, apply the minimum diff.**
Flat checkbox list, nothing mutates until Apply; at commit, one `ungroup_outputs` **only if** someone is leaving, one `group_outputs([head, ...joiners])` **only if** someone is joining, nothing on an empty diff. **Why:** per-toggle mutation would fire a teardown per tap. **Effort: small–medium.** **Roon:** `ungroup_outputs` tears a Squeezebox zone down on every call, so batching is correctness, not optimisation; `group_outputs` is a **union** and cannot shrink or detach (measured E3b/E7b), so removal has exactly one primitive.

**3. Name the anchor in the sheet, in words.**
Pinned, non-focusable header row: "Playing here: Study. Everything you check joins Study." **Why:** Roon's most destructive moment is currently invisible and therefore feels arbitrary; Crestron says it out loud and nobody complains. **Effort: small.** **Roon:** free — the head is already yours to choose.

**4. Two verbs, one call, order swapped: "Listen to the Kitchen here" vs "Add the Kitchen".**
Adopt = `group_outputs([kitchenOut, myOut])`; push = `group_outputs([myOut, kitchenOut])`. **Why:** it makes the queue-discard rule legible instead of a surprise, and it is Josh.ai's grammar for free. **Effort: small.** **Roon:** exactly the ordered array; no new primitive.

**5. Modal transport button: when this zone is grouped, Play/Pause becomes "Mute this room".**
With a visible muted state on the Face. **Why:** BluOS's answer to "what should a per-zone pause do when grouped" — the house keeps playing, this room goes quiet, and no group call is made at all. It is also the cheap substitute for a mid-song leave. **Effort: small.** **Roon:** per-output volume/mute is exposed; membership untouched, so no teardown.

**6. Room Off vs All Off as distinct verbs, plus topology-blind broadcast.**
Room Off = one `ungroup_outputs([thatOutput])`; All Off = **one** ungroup of the whole set + stop; "pause the house" acts on every zone and forms nothing. **Why:** Control4/Crestron ship exactly this, and the failure to distinguish them is what makes people issue N calls. **Effort: small.** **Roon:** never N per-room calls, never remove-then-re-add. Note the single-output ungroup is not yet reachable through FlightDeck's own route — `src/http/server.ts:506` is zone-wide; the `outputs` array passthrough is two lines.

**7. Saved groups as recipes, created by demonstration.**
Persist `{ordered output_ids + display names, per-output volume, optionally what to play}`. Create it by pressing "Save this grouping" on a group you already built (HEOS/Sonos/MusicCast all do this; nobody makes you re-pick rooms). Recall = resolve → filter → diff → minimum change. **Why:** the single most-requested missing Roon feature since 2017, and the recipe form is the only one that survives a room being absent. **Effort: medium.** **Roon:** entirely FlightDeck's object — Roon has no group identity and destroys custom group names on ungroup. Persist **both** id and name and reconcile by whichever resolves; `output_id` is stable across grouping, `zone_id` is not, and every `group_outputs` mints a new zone id — so own the group by "the zone containing headOutput", never by id.

**8. Two-tier absent-member handling, reported on the Face.**
Resolvable + awake → include. Resolvable but asleep → wake it, then include. Unresolvable → skip **and say so**: "Playing in 3 rooms — Kitchen was asleep." **Why:** Music Assistant is the only implementation of eight that splits "off" from "gone"; the other seven half-form, crash, or silently no-op. **Effort: medium.** **Roon:** exposes no availability flag at all, and `group_outputs` is atomic with no per-output result — one stale id fails the whole call and tells you nothing, so you must filter to a positively-confirmed set before issuing it. The wake knowledge has to come from RHEOS's own HEOS liveness, not from Roon. **This is also the one place a display beats every surveyed system** — it is the only surface that can close the loop.

**9. Group master volume as a ratio, over per-room trims, in one sheet — with pre-commit trims.**
Master applies a proportional change so room balance survives; trims are settable before Apply so a recall never blares in the bedroom. **Why:** it is the #1 standing Roon complaint (open since 2015) and the fix lives entirely in your layer. **Effort: medium.** **Roon:** there is no group-volume call; `change_volume` is per output and Roon warns outputs differ in type, range and step, so the master must fan out and reconcile heterogeneous scales itself, excluding fixed-volume outputs from the maths.

**10. One flat list, four row states, no tree; ineligible rooms dimmed and non-focusable.**
ANCHOR (pinned) / MEMBER / AVAILABLE / UNAVAILABLE. The list you can honestly render is `anchor.can_group_with_output_ids ∪ current members`; everything else goes in a non-focusable block. **Why:** every 10-foot system in the survey is focus-based, not hover-based, and a focusable dead row is a trap under nearest-neighbour focus movement. **Effort: small.** **Roon:** empty `can_group_with_output_ids` = "can't" (already `v1-hub.ts:278`). Never explain the partition in protocol terms — Roon names none, so any label you write is a guess.

**11. Auto-name the group after its anchor ("Study + 2"), collapse it to one expandable row.**
**Why:** the label is the receipt for which queue was kept, and it degrades gracefully as membership changes. **Effort: small.** **Roon:** free; keep FlightDeck's own custom name, which Roon would otherwise destroy at ungroup.

**12. Scheduled grouping — "at 07:30 form Upstairs and play."**
**Why:** unoccupied in every consumer ecosystem and table stakes in custom install; a saved group that is a first-class object inherits scheduling for nothing. **Effort: large.** **Roon:** composable from the recipe in #7 plus a timer; no API obstacle, just scope. Last, not first.

## What FlightDeck should NOT copy

- **Google's saved group as a real network object with an elected leader.** Impossible anyway (Roon has no group entity), and the failure mode is the lesson: the group is only as available as one arbitrary member, and Google's own troubleshooting is "restart everything, you can't tell which one is the leader." A recipe resolved at recall time is strictly better.
- **Per-toggle live mutation** (Apple, Google's picker, BluOS ad-hoc). Elegant on hardware that treats grouping as cheap. Here, every tick would be a `group_outputs` or a `ungroup_outputs`, and the latter tears a Squeezebox zone down each time. Stage and commit.
- **HEOS's pinch/spread and its drag-onto-drop-target.** The best gestures in the survey and both dead on arrival: the Samsung remote is a pointer that sleeps, Fire TV raises no motion events at all, and two-finger gestures have no D-pad equivalent. Keep the *direction-carries-the-anchor* idea, drop the drag.
- **BluOS Fixed Groups' collapse-and-hide.** A group that swallows its member rooms so they cannot be addressed individually is precisely the complaint Roon users already have; and FlightDeck cannot hide outputs from Roon anyway.
- **Alexa's "no way to leave mid-song".** An artefact of groups being cloud objects. You have per-room mute (#5) and a single-output ungroup; there is no reason to inherit the limitation.
- **LMS's "wait for next track to sync".** Beautiful cure for a problem Roon does not have — measured joins preserve position exactly (14→14, 233→233) and never restart the track. Building the machinery would be inventing a defect to fix.
- **The silent half-form** (HA Bluesound's bare `continue`, HA MusicCast's swallowed exception, node-sonos's `TypeError`). "Whole House" that quietly produces one room and reports success is the worst outcome available. Filter first, then one call, then report what was skipped.
- **Sonos's "group with no music context" as a feature.** Roon does produce a silent group from a non-playing head — but it is a *dead* one: `nowPlaying: null`, `allowed.play:false`. Never offer it as an intentional outcome.
- **A global "Everywhere" button.** Roon will not cross the protocol partition, so an all-rooms control lies about half the house. Everywhere means everywhere in *this* anchor's equivalence class; suppress it, Sonos-style, when that class is trivially small.
- **Announcing the cost of joining, Roon-style ("DSP will be disabled").** Correct instinct, impossible here: no format, resolution or signal-path data reaches an extension. Don't promise a warning you cannot compute.
- **Roon's self-concealing Group button.** Keep the affordance and say why the list is empty. Roon's version generates support threads.

## The one idea worth stealing

**The saved group should be a first-class named object that owns its own recipe, and it should assemble at play and disassemble at stop.** This is philippe44's LMS Group Players, and Music Assistant reached the same shape independently ("the group player holds the queue rather than any individual player"). Every other saved-group design in the survey is either a recipe you re-apply to live rooms (HEOS, Sonos, MusicCast) or a permanent fusion that eats its members (BluOS, Cast); the virtual-player model is the only one where the persistent thing and the live thing are cleanly separated — the object always exists, the *grouping* exists only while audio is flowing.

That separation is worth more to FlightDeck than to anyone who invented it, because of one measured asymmetry: `ungroup_outputs` tears a Squeezebox grouped zone down on every call, while `group_outputs` on a playing head is nearly free (queue and position preserved exactly, one ≤711 ms reporting flash). Assemble-on-play/disassemble-on-stop pays the expensive primitive **once per listening session** instead of once per fiddle, and it hands every room back the instant the music stops — which is the exact complaint behind Roon's nine-year-old "Save Grouped Zones" thread, where users say they want groups they can recall *without losing the ability to use the rooms individually*. Add the pause-breakup timeout the LMS plugin learned the hard way (hold the group assembled for N minutes after a pause purely so resume works, then dissolve) and the whole lifecycle is covered.

It also inherits. A group that is a *thing* on the House Wall — a row you press, with a name, a member list, a master volume and a "what it plays" — can carry a schedule, a volume set, a preset, and a "Kitchen was asleep" status line without any of those being special cases. Roon will never give you a zone object to hang this on; that is not a limitation here, it is the opportunity. Create it MusicCast's way — long-press the group you already built and it saves master, membership, source and volume together — so recipes accumulate from real listening rather than from an authoring chore.

## Confidence and gaps

**Solid.** Sonos, HEOS, Apple and Alexa are from vendor documentation, often verbatim; LMS/Material/Music Assistant/HA integrations/node-sonos were read as source, not docs; the Roon behavioural law is measured on the live Core across 8 runs and two protocol islands with stated denominators (paused head → empty group 5/5; playing head → position exact 2/2).

**Thin, and flagged as such.** ELAN is one integrator write-up plus a tutorial title — Nice's doc portal is JS-gated. HEOS Group Presets come from search snippets of an SPA that blocked fetch and has no Wayback copy; the creation flow is consistent across two passes but unverified. Volumio's group persistence is community-reported only. Plexamp grouping most likely does not exist — the SEO pages claiming "64 players, 50 ms" are uncorroborated by anything from Plex. Google's group-volume scaling semantics (proportional vs common level) are undocumented; the tidy model that surfaces in search is from a *Sonos* patent. Yamaha's unlink flow, Sonos's on-leave behaviour, and BluOS's on-leave behaviour are all inferred from mechanism, not stated.

**The universal blank.** Not one vendor documents what a saved group does when a member is powered off at recall. There is no prior art to copy — only implementations, and they disagree seven ways. The wake-if-asleep / skip-if-gone split is Music Assistant's alone.

**Open on the Roon side, and it matters.**
- `ungroup_outputs([oneOutput])` from a *live* group is still unmeasured through FlightDeck's own route — `src/http/server.ts:506` only expresses the zone-wide form. RHEOS runs the single-output form in production and its recorded outcome (remainder recreated stopped, needs one Roon-led play) is consistent, but it is not a measurement of mine.
- Why a playing head sometimes lands **paused** after grouping (1 of 2 runs) is unexplained; the only difference was whether the joining member held a conflicting queue. Worth n=3 each way before relying on form-and-play.
- One run (R3) had a paused head whose queue survived. It doesn't fit the law; both zones shared a queue lineage. Unresolved.
- Whether a Roon group survives its **head** sleeping: untested.
- The "cannot ungroup while a member is offline" lock is **retired** — it traces to a single 2018 report the reporter could not reproduce and asked to close, and 2025 Roon staff prescribe ungrouping as the remedy for exactly that state. That is absence of evidence, not proof; if you rely on it, rely on it knowingly.
- The genuinely live hazard replacing it is the **network-standby member**: a room in standby is visible, passes every check the API can perform, and wedges the whole group including healthy members (ref#RW7480, Roon 2.x, no fix, ungroup prescribed). Your house is entirely made of that case. The eco-standby room that vanishes from the output list is the *safe* one.
- Whether the 1.x extension-API zone-serialisation NPE (a powered-down endpoint in a grouped zone spamming the Core until restart, topic 54530) survives into 2.x is unproven. RHEOS's modern measurement is the cleaner `ZoneNotFound` rejection, which suggests it was fixed — but the class of hazard lands on the zone subscription every Face depends on, so every call gets a callback.
